# Cross Stitch — Privacy Policy

_Last updated 21 November 2026_


## The short version

Cross Stitch collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.
Everything you make stays on your device.

## Photos

When you choose a photo, the app copies it into its own private storage on
your device so the pattern can show where it came from. It only sees the
photos you pick. Deleting a pattern project deletes its copy of the photo.

## Your projects and threads

Pattern projects, stitching progress, thread amounts and your thread stash are
saved in the app's private storage on your device. Nobody else, including the
developer, can see them.

## Backups

Like other apps' documents, this storage can be included in your device
backups (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are
governed by your own settings and Apple's privacy policy.

## Removing your data

Delete a pattern project from its menu, or delete the app to remove
everything it stored.

## Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear in the app and on
the App Store page, with a new date.

## Thread colours

Thread colours are referenced by DMC number so you know which floss to buy.
Cross Stitch is not affiliated with or endorsed by DMC.
